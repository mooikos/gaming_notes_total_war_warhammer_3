# Armies Compositions

## Tabletop Caps Rules

- **Army Size:** 20 units (`1` Lord + `3-4` Heroes + `15-16` units)
- **Common (Core):** Unlimited (`0` points) — typically `6-8` units per army
- **Special:** Max **10 points** (`Special 1` = 1 pt, `Special 2` = 2 pts, `Special 3` = 3 pts) — typically `4-8` units per army
- **Rare:** Max **5 points** (`Rare 1` = 1 pt, `Rare 2` = 2 pts, `Rare 3` = 3 pts) — typically `2-4` units per army

## Standard Hero Set

Between 3 and 5 characters (including the Lord):

- 1 tank
- 1 fighter
- (optional) 1 ranged
- (optional) 1 support
- 1 mage

## Ranged Waiting

Hold a defensive line and flank guards while missile infantry and long-range artillery force the enemy to advance into crossfire.

- 1 [standard hero set](#standard-hero-set)
- **Common (8):**
  - 4 defensive `infantry_melee`
  - 4 `infantry_missile`
- **Special (5–6 — 10 pts):**
  - 2-3 offensive / anti-large `infantry_melee`
  - 2-3 `infantry_missile` / `cavalry_melee` / `monstrous_cavalry`
- **Rare (3–4 — 5 pts):**
  - 2-3 `artilleries`
  - 0-1 `monsters` / `war_machines`

## Ranged Offensive

Advance into range with armoured or hybrid frontline units and high-damage missile infantry that focus down priority targets before engaging in melee.

- 1 [standard hero set](#standard-hero-set)
- **Common (8):**
  - 4 offensive / armoured `infantry_melee`
  - 4 skirmish / shielded `infantry_missile`
- **Special (5–6 — 10 pts):**
  - 3-4 elite / stealth `infantry_missile`
  - 2 `cavalry_melee` / `monstrous_infantry` / `monsters`
- **Rare (2–3 — 5 pts):**
  - 1-2 ranged `monsters` / `war_machines`
  - 1 melee `monsters`

## Melee Engage

Close the distance rapidly with high-damage melee infantry and monstrous infantry, supported by buff war machines and fast flankers.

- 1 [standard hero set](#standard-hero-set)
- **Common (8):**
  - 4-6 offensive `infantry_melee`
  - 2-4 `infantry_missile` / `cavalry_missile`
- **Special (5–6 — 10 pts):**
  - 3-4 elite `infantry_melee` / `monstrous_infantry`
  - 2 `cavalry_melee` / `cavalry_melee_shock` / `chariots`
- **Rare (3–4 — 5 pts):**
  - 2-3 elite `infantry_melee` / `monsters`
  - 1 support `war_machines`

## Hammer & Anvil

Pin the enemy line in place with a sturdy Common anvil while shock cavalry, monstrous cavalry, and chariots smash the flanks and rear.

- 1 [standard hero set](#standard-hero-set)
- **Common (8):**
  - 4 defensive `infantry_melee`
  - 4 `infantry_missile`
- **Special (5–6 — 10 pts):**
  - 4-6 `cavalry_melee_shock` / `monstrous_cavalry` / `chariots`
- **Rare (2–3 — 5 pts):**
  - 2-3 `monsters` / `war_machines`

## Skirmish & Kite

Pull the enemy formation apart with fast missile cavalry, light cavalry, and fire-whilst-moving skirmishers, then isolate and destroy separated units.

- 1 [standard hero set](#standard-hero-set)
- **Common (8):**
  - 4 `cavalry_missile` / skirmish `infantry_missile`
  - 4 fast `cavalry_melee` / `infantry_melee`
- **Special (5–6 — 10 pts):**
  - 2 ranged / melee `chariots`
  - 3-4 stealth `infantry_missile` / `cavalry_melee`
- **Rare (2–3 — 5 pts):**
  - 2-3 fast `monsters` / `flying_monsters`

## Vanguard & Ambush

Use `vanguard_deployment`, `stalk`, and `flying_*` / `war_beasts` to collapse on the enemy from all directions and shut down their backline immediately.

- 1 [standard hero set](#standard-hero-set)
- **Common (6–8):**
  - 4 fast offensive `infantry_melee` / `cavalry_melee`
  - 2-4 skirmish `infantry_missile` / `cavalry_missile`
- **Special (6–8 — 10 pts):**
  - 4 `war_beasts` / `flying_war_beasts`
  - 2-4 stealth `infantry_missile` / `flying_monsters`
- **Rare (3–4 — 5 pts):**
  - 2-3 fast elite `infantry_melee` / `monsters`
  - 1 `war_machines` / `flying_monsters`

## Monster & Beast Pack

Maximize single-entity `monsters` and `war_beasts` within the Special and Rare caps to break enemy leadership with `fear` and `terror`, backed by a Common infantry screen.

- 1 [standard hero set](#standard-hero-set)
- **Common (8):**
  - 4 `infantry_melee`
  - 4 `infantry_missile`
- **Special (6–8 — 10 pts):**
  - 4 `war_beasts` / `flying_war_beasts`
  - 2-4 `monsters` / `monstrous_infantry` / `chariots`
- **Rare (2–3 — 5 pts):**
  - 2-3 `monsters` / `flying_monsters`

## Siege & Assault

Crack fortified settlements or entrenched positions using artillery, gate-breaking monsters, heavily armoured melee infantry for wall/street grinding, and arcing missiles.

- 1 [standard hero set](#standard-hero-set)
- **Common (8):**
  - 4 shielded `infantry_melee`
  - 4 arcing `infantry_missile`
- **Special (5 — 10 pts):**
  - 5 armoured `infantry_melee` / `monstrous_infantry`
- **Rare (3 — 5 pts):**
  - 2 `artilleries`
  - 1 `monsters`
