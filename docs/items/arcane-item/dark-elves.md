# Arcane Item (Dark Elves)

Available to all **Dark Elves** factions (including **The Blessed Dread** / `lokhir_fellheart`).
*Note: Equipped by spellcaster Lords and Heroes (`supreme_sorceress`, `sorceress`, `malekith`, `morathi`); `lokhir_fellheart` himself is a melee Lord and does not have an Arcane Item slot.*

| Item | Rarity | Stats | Ability & Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `darkstar_cloak` | Common | `stalk` | **`darkstar_cloak`** (passive, *Augment of the Winds*, `self`, infinite duration): Passively grants `+40% power_recharge`. |
| `scroll_of_lykos` *(multi-race)* | Common | `+10% movement_speed` | **`speed_of_lykos`** (active, *Hiding*, `target` ally within 200m, 1 use, 36s duration, 60s cooldown): Buffs target allied unit with `+25% movement_speed`, `stalk`, and `unspottable`. |
| `tome_of_furion` | Common | `+10% spells_range` | **`tome_of_furion`** (bound spell, *Bombardment*, `target` enemy within 200m, 4 uses, 7s duration, 60s cooldown): Calls down 1 bolt of Chaos black lightning dealing `300` (100% armour-piercing) `attacks_magic` ranged damage in a medium strike area (strong vs single combatant / single unit). |
| `black_staff` | Uncommon | `+10% spell_intensity` | **`black_staff`** (bound spell, *Augment of the Winds*, `target` ally within 200m, 3 uses, 20s duration, 90s cooldown): Grants `+80% power_recharge` and `+0.70 power_added` reserves per second, while dealing `16-33` direct damage per second to self/target. |
